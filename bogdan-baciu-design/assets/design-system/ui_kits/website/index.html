Section · 02 / 07

A long lede sets the scene. It runs as italic display serif, justified with text-wrap pretty, sized for comfortable reading. This paragraph is about thirty-five words long and gives the rest of the column permission to breathe.


Body type lands at 20px on a 1.78 line height, capped at seventy-four characters because anything wider becomes uncomfortable to track. Soft hyphenation kicks in at ten characters, which keeps abbreviations and short words intact while letting compound words break and prevent rivers in justified prose1.

Links pick up the sky accent and a one-pixel underline. On hover they paint a sky-100 wash behind the text — same gesture the row tables use, so the whole site feels stitched from one fabric. The accent italic inside body copy is the only place the brand color appears in prose.

This bio section uses position: sticky on a 4:5 portrait at the left. The reading column flows past it; the portrait stays put until the section bottom catches it. Page padding is 36px horizontal — not 32, not 40.

  1. FN 1. Hyphenation values are tuned in flourishes.css §22 — adjust there, not inline.
